# Prompt do ícone orçamento.

Arte criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (image_gen). Exportações redimensionadas com Sharp, preservando a arte original.

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE final app icon artwork for the Brazilian personal budget app 'orçamento.'. Square 1024 x 1024 pixels. Full bleed opaque solid deep forest green #063F31 background, square outer corners (operating systems apply their own mask). Center a striking very simple flat geometric lime #CEF29B symbol: three thick vertical capsule bars ascending left to right, with a separate small circular light-green #92CA7B dot at the lower right aligned to the bars' baseline, referencing the brand's period. Three bars have fully rounded ends, generous even spacing, clean optical balance, middle and right bars progressively taller; no arrow. All the symbol, including dot, must fit within the central 58% of the canvas and within the central circle of radius 36% of canvas width, ample breathing room suitable for circular app icon masks. Precise minimal vector-like silhouette, crisp edges, modern premium calm finance identity, instantly readable at favicon size. No text, no letters, no currency sign, no numbers, no outlines, no gradients, no texture, no shadows, no mockup, no device frame, no border, no extra icons, no white outer margins. Render only the flat finished icon artwork.
